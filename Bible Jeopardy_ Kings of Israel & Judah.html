How to Play

  • Divide the group into teams. Teams take turns choosing a category and point value.
  • Read the clue aloud. Give teams a short time to discuss before they answer.
  • Click Reveal Answer to show the response and Scripture reference. Award the points for a correct answer; subtract points for an incorrect answer if you choose.
  • Click Mark Complete / Close Question to remove that clue from play.
  • After the board is finished, play Final Jeopardy. Let teams wager points before revealing the answer.
  • Use the Scripture references to look up the story together. Ask: “What does this teach us about following God?”

Leader tip: Keep the focus on more than remembering names. Invite students to notice how a king's choices affected the people—and how faith, humility, and obedience mattered.
