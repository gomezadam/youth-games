Category: The Gospel Message

According to 1 Corinthians 15, what three events form the central summary of the gospel that Paul says he received and passed on?

SCRIPTURE: 1 CORINTHIANS 15:1–5
Answer: Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, was buried, and was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures.

Suggested Game Rules

  1. Divide the congregation into two or more teams.
  2. Teams take turns selecting a category and point value.
  3. Give teams 15–30 seconds to answer.
  4. Require answers in the form of a question, just like Jeopardy.
  5. Award points for correct answers and deduct points for incorrect answers.
  6. The team with the highest score wins!

A great teaching addition: After each answer, have someone read the corresponding New Testament passage aloud. This turns the game into a Bible study rather than simply a trivia competition.
