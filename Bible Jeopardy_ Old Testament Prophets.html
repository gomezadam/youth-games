How to Play

  • Divide the group into teams. Teams take turns choosing a category and point value.
  • Read the clue aloud. Give teams a short time to discuss before they answer.
  • Click Reveal Answer to show the response and Scripture reference. Award points for a correct answer; subtract points for an incorrect answer if you choose.
  • Click Mark Complete / Close Question to remove that clue from play.
  • After the board is finished, play Final Jeopardy. Let teams wager points before revealing the answer.
  • Use the Scripture references to read the stories together. Ask students what each prophet's story teaches about listening to and trusting God.

Leader tip: This game is designed to be relatively easy. Encourage students to learn the big stories and remember that prophets delivered God's messages, even when the message was difficult.
