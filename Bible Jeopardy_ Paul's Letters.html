Suggested Game Rules

  1. Divide the congregation into two or more teams.
  2. Teams take turns selecting a category and point value.
  3. Give teams 15–30 seconds to answer.
  4. Require answers in the form of a question, just like Jeopardy.
  5. Award points for correct answers and deduct points for incorrect answers.
  6. The team with the highest score wins!

A great teaching addition: After each answer, have someone read the corresponding Bible passage aloud. This turns the game into a Bible study rather than simply a trivia competition.
