Bonus Final Jeopardy

FINAL JEOPARDY · 1,000 POINTS

Category: Faith That Trusts

According to Hebrews 11, what did Abraham do when God called him to go to a place he would later receive as an inheritance—even though he did not know where he was going?

SCRIPTURE: HEBREWS 11:8
Answer: By faith, he obeyed and went, even though he did not know where he was going.

Suggested Game Rules

  1. Divide the youth group into two or more teams.
  2. Teams take turns choosing a category and point value.
  3. Give teams 15–30 seconds to answer. For tougher questions, allow a little extra time.
  4. Have players answer in the form of a question, just like Jeopardy.
  5. Award points for correct answers and deduct points for incorrect answers.
  6. The team with the highest score wins!

A great teaching addition: After each answer, ask the group what the story teaches about trusting God, making choices, and following Him even when the future is uncertain.
