Bonus Final Jeopardy

FINAL JEOPARDY · 1,000 POINTS

Category: God's Covenant

In Jeremiah 31, what new covenant did God promise to make with the house of Israel and the house of Judah, and where would he write his law?

SCRIPTURE: JEREMIAH 31:31–34
Answer: A new covenant in which God would put his law within them and write it on their hearts.

Suggested Game Rules

  1. Divide the congregation into two or more teams.
  2. Teams take turns selecting a category and point value.
  3. Give teams 15–30 seconds to answer.
  4. Require answers in the form of a question, just like Jeopardy.
  5. Award points for correct answers and deduct points for incorrect answers.
  6. The team with the highest score wins!

A great teaching addition: After each answer, have someone read the corresponding Old Testament passage aloud. This turns the game into a Bible study rather than simply a trivia competition.
