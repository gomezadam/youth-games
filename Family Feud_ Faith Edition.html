How to Play

1. The Face-Off: Two teams buzz in to give a top survey answer. Whoever finds the higher-ranked answer chooses to Play or Pass the board.

2. Clearing the Board: The controlling team guesses answers to build the Round Bank. Wrong guesses earn a Strike (X).

3. Three Strikes & The Steal: After 3 strikes, the opposing team gets one chance to steal the entire Round Bank with any remaining unrevealed answer!

4. Multipliers & Fast Money: Rounds 1–2 pay 1x, Round 3 pays 2x, and Round 4 pays 3x. The winning team advances to the 60-second Fast Money bonus round to reach 200 points!
