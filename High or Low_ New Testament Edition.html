How to Play

Test your knowledge of the Gospels, Acts, the Epistles, and Revelation in this Greco-Roman manuscript challenge!

  • 1. Every round compares two New Testament entries using the exact same unit of measurement (Chapters, Days, Years, People, or Quantities).

  • 3. Predict whether the Challenger Scroll (Right) has a HIGHER or LOWER value. Equal values (ties) count as correct!
